# Phase 4.1: Interview Preparation & Guide

> [!NOTE]
> **Objective:** Validate the AI-generated insights regarding **Sizing Uncertainty** with real human users. This document defines our target user segment and outlines a structured interview guide for Sub-Phase 4.2.

## 1. Target User Segment
Based on the discovery insights from Phase 2 and 3, we need to recruit 5-6 participants who fit the following criteria:

- **Demographic:** Regular e-commerce fashion shoppers (e.g., Millennials/Gen Z) who actively use or have used the AJIO app.
- **Behavioral (The "Hesitant Wishlister"):** Users who frequently add clothing items to their wishlist but routinely leave them there for extended periods without purchasing.
- **Psychographic:** Users who have expressed past frustration with online sizing, fit expectations, or the operational friction of returning items. 

## 2. Google Form Survey Template (Sub-Phase 4.2)
*Goal: To quickly validate that "Sizing Uncertainty" is the primary purchase blocker at scale using multiple-choice questions.*

**Q1: When shopping on AJIO, why do you usually wishlist an item instead of buying it immediately?**
- [ ] Waiting for a price drop or sale
- [ ] Unsure if the size or fit will look good on me
- [ ] Want to compare it with other options
- [ ] Need to ask friends/family for opinions
- [ ] Other: ______

**Q2: When you hesitate to buy an item on AJIO because of sizing, what information is usually missing?**
- [ ] Realistic photos of normal people wearing it
- [ ] The exact height/weight measurements of the model
- [ ] A reliable, easy-to-understand size chart
- [ ] Information about how stretchy or tight the fabric is
- [ ] Other: ______

**Q3: How do you currently try to figure out if an AJIO item will fit before buying?**
- [ ] Read customer reviews
- [ ] Look at customer photos uploaded in reviews
- [ ] Search for YouTube or Instagram hauls of the brand
- [ ] Order two sizes and return the one that doesn't fit
- [ ] I just guess and hope for the best

**Q4: How much does the fear of AJIO's return process stop you from buying an item?**
- [ ] A lot (I often abandon items just to avoid the return hassle)
- [ ] Somewhat (It makes me hesitate, but I usually buy eventually)
- [ ] Not at all (I don't mind returning things)

**Q5: Which fashion app makes you feel the MOST confident about choosing the right size?**
