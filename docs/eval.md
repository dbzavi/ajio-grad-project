# AI-Powered Discovery Engine: Evaluation Criteria

This document defines the evaluation criteria (evals) for each phase of the **Wishlist to Purchase Conversion** project as outlined in `implementation-plan.md`. These criteria serve as Go/No-Go checkpoints to ensure quality and relevance before moving to the next phase.

---

## Phase 1: Environment Setup & Data Ingestion
**Goal:** Build a robust, relevant dataset from public forums and app reviews.

### Evaluation Criteria (Go/No-Go)
- **Data Volume (Quantitative):** At least 1,000 unique reviews/comments successfully scraped and stored in `raw_data.csv`/`raw_data.json`.
- **Data Relevance (Qualitative):** A random sample of 50 rows shows that at least 70% of the data pertains directly to online fashion shopping, wishlist behaviors, cart abandonment, or price/fit concerns.
- **Data Integrity:** No duplicated entries. Null values and completely empty text fields are successfully filtered out.
- **Anonymization Check:** The dataset does not contain raw emails, phone numbers, or unmasked PII.

---

## Phase 2: Processing, Analytics & AI Discovery
**Goal:** Use the Antigravity LLM to categorize the raw data into actionable themes.

### Evaluation Criteria (Go/No-Go)
- **Execution Success:** The LLM successfully processes the entire dataset without crashing due to context window limits.
- **Categorization Clarity (Qualitative):** The AI-generated themes (e.g., Sizing Uncertainty, Saving for Inspiration) are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive (MECE) based on the data provided.
- **Verifiability:** Every major insight generated by the AI includes at least 3 verbatim quotes from the `raw_data` to prove it did not hallucinate.
- **Quantification (Quantitative):** The output `discovery_insights.md` ranks the top themes by percentage occurrence across the dataset (e.g., "45% mentioned size doubt").

---

## Phase 3: Metric Decomposition & Target Selection
**Goal:** Connect qualitative insights to specific, non-monetary business metrics.

### Evaluation Criteria (Go/No-Go)
- **Constraint Adherence (Critical):** The selected focus area strictly avoids monetary solutions (e.g., discounts, sales, coupons).
- **Logical Flow:** The mapped leading indicators clearly connect to the overarching "Wishlist to Purchase Conversion" rate.
- **Actionability:** The chosen target opportunity is something that product, engineering, or UX teams can influence (e.g., UI layout, social sharing, size guides).

---

## Phase 4: Primary User Research & Problem Definition
**Goal:** Validate AI findings with real humans and formalize the exact problem statement.

### Evaluation Criteria (Go/No-Go)
- **Sample Size & Quality:** Successfully conducted 5-6 interviews with users who fit the target segment profile.
- **Hypothesis Validation:** The interviews either confirm the AI's top hypothesis OR explicitly invalidate it (in which case, the pivot must be documented).
- **Problem Statement Quality:** The final Problem Definition clearly articulates:
  1. **The User:** Who is experiencing the problem?
  2. **The Need:** What are they trying to do?
  3. **The Friction:** Why can't they do it?
  4. **The Impact:** Why does solving this matter to the business?

---

## Phase 5: MVP Ideation, Build & Deployment
**Goal:** Build and deploy a testable solution targeting the defined problem.

### Evaluation Criteria (Go/No-Go)
- **Feature Traceability:** The MVP features map directly to the defined problem statement (no scope creep or irrelevant features).
- **Functionality (Quantitative):** The MVP runs locally and remotely without breaking (0 fatal errors on the main user flow).
- **Accessibility/Deployment:** The MVP is deployed to a live URL (e.g., Vercel, Streamlit Cloud) and is accessible on both desktop and mobile web browsers.
- **Simplicity (MVP constraint):** The solution can be explained and demonstrated in under 2 minutes.

---

## Phase 6: Deck Assembly & Final Deliverables
**Goal:** Synthesize the entire project into a 10-slide presentation.

### Evaluation Criteria (Go/No-Go)
- **Length Constraint:** The deck is exactly 10 slides (excluding title/appendix if permitted, but the core narrative must fit in 10).
- **Formatting Constraints:** Adheres strictly to the guidelines (e.g., minimum font size 14, color-blind friendly palettes, easily scannable text).
- **Anonymity Constraint:** No candidate names or personal identifiers are present on the slides.
- **Narrative Arc:** The presentation clearly bridges the gap between raw data (Phase 1) and the final deployed MVP (Phase 5), effectively answering the core questions from the initial prompt.
- **Artifact Inclusion:** All deliverables (links to MVP, Discovery Engine scripts, `insights.md`) are hyperlinked and functioning within the PDF export.
