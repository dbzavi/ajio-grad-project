# Phase 4.3: Problem Definition

> [!NOTE]
> **Objective:** Formally define the core problem we are solving based on quantitative survey validation and AI discovery insights.

## The Problem Statement
**"High-intent shoppers on AJIO are abandoning their wishlists because they lack confidence in product sizing/fit and fear the friction of the return process."**

---

## 1. The Root Cause (Why is this happening?)
Based on our 30-user quantitative survey and 1,000+ AI-analyzed reviews, the primary blocker to wishlist conversion is **Sizing Uncertainty**. 
- **55%** of surveyed users stated that fit uncertainty is their primary reason for wishlisting instead of buying immediately.
- **50%** of users indicated that the fear of returning an item "a lot" stops them from completing a purchase, leading to cart abandonment.
- Users currently rely heavily on *customer review photos* (35%) rather than official size charts to estimate fit, highlighting a severe trust deficit in brand-provided measurements.

## 2. Existing Workarounds (What do they do today?)
Because users cannot trust the standard size charts, they resort to high-friction external workflows:
- **Review Mining:** Scouring customer reviews specifically to find photos of normal people wearing the item.
- **Over-Ordering (Bracketing):** Ordering two different sizes with the strict intent of returning the one that doesn't fit (which incurs massive reverse-logistics costs for AJIO).
- **Competitor Reliance:** 60% of surveyed users feel more confident about sizing on Myntra because of better user-generated photos and automated size recommenders.

## 3. User Value (Why does the user care?)
If we solve this problem, the user benefits by:
- Eliminating the anxiety of buying clothes online.
- Saving time spent hunting for YouTube hauls or external reviews just to guess their size.
- Avoiding the tedious, frustrating process of packing up boxes and waiting for return pickups.

## 4. Business Value (Why does AJIO care?)
If we solve this problem, the business benefits by:
- **Direct Revenue Increase:** Unlocking stranded revenue by migrating stagnant wishlist items directly into the checkout cart.
- **Drastic Cost Reduction:** Reducing the frequency of "bracketing" (ordering multiple sizes to return one), which slashes reverse logistics and warehouse restocking costs.
- **Retention:** Winning back sizing trust from competitors like Myntra.
