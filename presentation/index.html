Unlocking Wishlist Revenue by Eliminating "Sizing Uncertainty" at the Point of Conversion

The Problem

48% of high-intent wishlisters abandon their purchase due to uncertainty around how a garment will actually fit their specific body type.

The Discovery

A custom AI Engine analyzed 1,000+ fashion reviews, revealing that users trust other users with similar measurements over generic size charts.

The Solution (MVP)

AJIO Fit-Match AI

A dual-trust widget using RAG to map reviews to the user's specific body profile, boosting conversion without monetary incentives.

Deconstructing the Funnel: Why 'Sizing Confidence' is the Highest Leverage Opportunity

1. Add to Wishlist (High Intent)

2. Return to App (Re-engagement)

3. Evaluate Fit/Price (The Bottleneck)

4. Checkout (Conversion)

Why focus on Fit over Price?

The prompt constrains us from using monetary incentives. Sizing uncertainty is the single largest friction point blocking Step 3 to Step 4. By providing extreme sizing confidence, we bypass the need for discounts entirely.

AI Analysis Reveals "Sizing Paranoia" as the Primary Driver of Abandonment

Top Non-Monetary Friction Points:

  1. Sizing inconsistency across brands (48% of mentions)
  2. Lack of visual representation on average bodies (32% of mentions)
  3. Fabric quality doubts (20% of mentions)

The Core Insight

Users use the wishlist as a "bookmark" while they hunt for external validation (YouTube hauls, Reddit reviews) to confirm fit. If they can't find validation, the item stays wishlisted forever.

Proactively Mitigating AI Hallucinations and UI Clutter

Risk 1: LLM Hallucinations (Bad Size Advice)

Mitigation: Strict RAG boundaries. The AI only triggers if >5 highly relevant reviews exist for the SKU. Otherwise, gracefully fallback.

Risk 2: Low Feature Discoverability

Mitigation: Implement micro-animations (glow effects) on the widget if the user lingers on the size-selector tool for >3 seconds.
